Project: Apple Financial Performance Analysis

Objective:
Analyze sales and profitability performance across months, countries, products, and business segments.

Key Findings
Total sales were $118.73M, with $16.89M total profit and a 14.23% profit margin.
October recorded the highest monthly sales, while sales were considerably lower in several other months.
Paseo was the highest-performing product by both sales and profit.
Government generated the highest sales among segments.
Channel Partners recorded the highest profit margin.
Enterprise had a negative profit margin, despite generating significant sales.
France recorded the highest total profit among the countries analyzed.
Recommendations
Investigate the causes of Enterprise's negative profitability.
Analyze what makes Channel Partners highly profitable and identify practices that could be replicated.
Maintain focus on Paseo, given its strong sales and profit performance.
Investigate the factors behind the October sales peak.
Evaluate segments using both sales and profitability, rather than sales alone.
Conclusion

The analysis shows that strong sales do not necessarily translate into strong profitability. While Government leads in sales, Channel Partners demonstrates superior margin performance, whereas Enterprise requires immediate profitability investigation.
